# درس ۱ — ولتاژ، جریان و توان (1.1 + 1.2)

Source: `books/aoe/pages/ch01/text.md`, PDF pages 1-12. Lesson: `site/aoe/ch01/index.html`,
narration `content/aoe/ch01/narration.fa.json`, audio `site/aoe/ch01/audio/fa/`.

## Scope chosen from the source
- 1.1 Introduction: what electronics is, why the first chapter is the most abstract and most
  mathematical, voltage/current as the two quantities worth tracking. The lesson keeps this to
  one "why" beat, because a lesson that opens on the definitions serves the learner better.
- 1.2.1 Voltage and current: the definitions, the units, the water-pressure analogy, the
  important "current *through* / voltage *across*" language rule, the conservation of charge
  and Kirchhoff's two laws, instantaneous power `P = VI`.
- Left out of this lesson (later lessons): Ω and Ohm's law, series/parallel resistors, voltage
  dividers, Thévenin, the prefixes table, small-signal resistance. They need the resistance
  machinery that section 1.2.2 builds, and lesson 1 stops at the two quantities themselves.

## Answers verified in the lesson
- `I = 0.6 C ÷ 2 s = 0.3 A`; `Q = 0.5 A × 4 s = 2 C` (q1).
- `P = 12 V × 0.5 A = 6 W`; `E = 6 W × 2 h = 12 Wh` (q2).
- `Q = 0.1 A × 10 s = 1 C` (practice 1).
- `P = 120 V × 0.5 A = 60 W` (beat 11, the book's own lamp example).
- `2 A × 300 s = 600 C`; `600 C = 1/6 Ah ≈ 0.167 Ah` (practice 2).
- True/false rows: current = charge/second (true), P = V·I (true), a voltage *at a point* alone
  (false — a voltage is always between two points), series current everywhere equal (true).
